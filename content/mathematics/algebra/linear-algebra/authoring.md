# Linear Algebra: authoring notes

Not rendered; `syllabus.md` is what learners see. Status: syllabus approved 2026-11-02 (proposed 2026-10-07); chapter 1 written, chapters 2 to 14 and the epilogue are planned flights with no lessons.

## Sources

- Jim Hefferon, *Linear Algebra*, 4th ed. (`~/Downloads/linalgebra.pdf`, 525 pp). Proof-first, abstract vector spaces in ch. 2, Jordan form in ch. 5, assumes a semester of calculus. Best for proofs and puzzle exercises (marked `?`). License is on his website, not in the PDF; recalled as share-alike. Nothing adapted yet; verify before borrowing any exercise.
- David Austin, *Understanding Linear Algebra* (`~/Downloads/Understanding Linear Algebra.pdf`, 519 pp). No calculus, stays in R^n, ends at SVD and PCA. CC BY 4.0 (verified, p. 2). Primary source for the arc and applied examples; credited as further reading at the end of `reading.md` (ch. 1 maps to Austin 2.1, 2.3, 2.4). The PDF is not redistributed.

## Rules

- Each chapter is one flight of 3 to 5 lessons. Lesson mix per `plugins/plato/skills/plato/SKILL.md`: confirm, break, unify kinds; about 1 in 4 lessons a trap; at least one human-only exercise per lesson.
- The explanation lives once, in `reading.md`; lessons hold only the trap, its answer key, and the case-specific Repair. The book is public, so its sections explain ideas with their own examples and never contain a lesson's Commit numbers or Truth.
- Interactive artifacts matter most in chapters 3, 4, 8, 12, and 14, where the claim is about a picture.

## Decisions

1. All 14 chapters and the epilogue kept, in this order. Merging 9 with 10, or 13 into 12, stays an option if the middle drags.
